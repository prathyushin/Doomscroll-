# Doom Scroll v2.0.0 — Major Release Blueprint

**Status:** Development started  
**Branch:** `v2.0.0-development`  
**Baseline:** `v1.2.1-privacy-hardening`  
**Product promise:** Your social media. Your rules.

## Release objective

v2.0.0 is a major product evolution of the existing Instagram + Threads WebView shell. It must preserve direct access to the social platforms while adding optional, local-first controls. The app must not become a mandatory dashboard, a replacement social network, or an intrusive focus app.

## Non-negotiable UX principles

- Launch directly into the last/default social platform.
- Keep Instagram and Threads browsing as the primary experience.
- Keep the existing floating, auto-hiding glass dock; refine rather than replace it.
- Controls are optional, reversible and unobtrusive.
- No forced onboarding, purpose selection or mandatory dashboard.
- Never claim control over server-side ranking or platform features that the WebView cannot reliably control.

## v2.0.0 workstreams

### 0. Foundation and stability (first)
- Use the v1.2.1 privacy-hardened WebView branch as the baseline.
- Keep the old AccessibilityService and earlier API-client experiments isolated.
- Fix and regression-test Threads startup/reload behaviour.
- Verify Instagram/Threads session persistence and Android Back handling.
- Verify Story muting and the existing dock on a physical device.
- Keep privacy hardening: no usage history or notification-listener content collection unless separately redesigned and explicitly approved.

### 1. Feed Firewall (approved)
- Independent Instagram and Threads settings.
- Hide explicitly identifiable recommendations and selected accounts where the site DOM exposes them.
- Category controls, including Reels, with a clear on/off state.
- Local, best-effort filtering only; selectors must fail safely and must not break core browsing.

### 2. Personal Feed Profiles (approved)
- One-tap profiles: Friends First, Clean Feed, Photo Mode, Focus Mode and Normal Mode.
- Profiles are presets over the user's Feed Firewall and focus preferences.
- Make profile switching available from the existing dock/control surface; no new mandatory landing page.

### 3. Personal Content Filters (approved)
- Configure when needed, then apply in the background.
- Avoid adding a permanent navigation destination to the normal browsing experience.
- Start with transparent, deterministic filters before attempting semantic classification.

### 4. Digital Wellbeing / Focus Controls (approved category)
- Smart schedules and quiet hours.
- Optional session limits/reminders.
- Finite-feed/manual-loading controls where technically feasible.
- Separate Story and post muting preferences.
- Optional Reels Speed Bump.
- Optional checkpoints remain off by default and must not interrupt ordinary DM use.

### 5. Privacy Dashboard (approved)
- Explain local filtering and ad-filtering status.
- Show Android permissions and what they are used for.
- Explain what data is stored locally.
- Analytics remain off unless a separate, explicit informed-consent design is approved.
- No precise GPS, contacts, passwords, private messages or browsing-history collection.

### 6. Smart Notification Centre (approved, capability-gated)
- Per-platform preferences, important-only choices, quiet hours and summaries.
- Clearly distinguish Android-native notifications from WebView notifications.
- Do not promise WebView push delivery unless verified and supported.

### 7. Accessibility and Comfort (approved)
- Text scaling, reduced motion, contrast, one-handed navigation and display comfort.

### 8. Community Feature Voting (approved concept)
- Suggestions and votes.
- Transparent statuses: under consideration, planned, in development and released.
- Any server-side voting backend requires a separate privacy and abuse-prevention design.

### 9. Learning Focus (proposed; behaviour unresolved)
- Preserve relevant educational search content while reducing unrelated recommendations.
- Platform ranking and search are server-controlled; local relevance detection is best-effort.
- Do not blanket-block educational Reels.
- Final behaviour choice is pending: relevant-results-only, relevant results plus optional stopping point, or user-controlled topic/session.

## Explicit exclusions
- Purpose Mode is rejected and must not be reintroduced.
- Scroll checkpoints must remain optional and off by default.
- No HTTPS interception/decryption for ad filtering.
- No private Meta endpoints, undocumented APIs, credential collection or scraping.
- No undisclosed analytics or telemetry.
- No forced dashboard or recurring in-feed nagging.

## Implementation sequence

1. Stabilize the v1.2.1 baseline and reproduce/resolve Threads reload issues.
2. Verify Story mute, sessions, navigation and dock behaviour.
3. Implement a tested Feed Firewall policy model and per-provider preferences.
4. Connect Personal Feed Profiles to those preferences.
5. Add background content filters.
6. Build consolidated Focus Controls.
7. Add Privacy Dashboard and capability-gated notification settings.
8. Add Accessibility/Comfort controls.
9. Design Community Voting and Learning Focus after requirements are finalized.
10. Complete physical-device regression, privacy review, release signing and release notes.

## Release gates

- CI unit tests and debug/release assembly pass on the v2 branch.
- Physical-device tests cover login, cookies, navigation, Threads startup, Story mute, dock, fullscreen and media picker.
- Feed filters are tested independently on Instagram and Threads and fail safely after DOM changes.
- Every optional control is reversible and does not block normal browsing unexpectedly.
- Privacy/permission behaviour is documented and opt-in.
- APK/AAB signing, checksums, version metadata and upgrade behaviour are verified.
- Release notes distinguish implemented, best-effort, pending verification and not supported features.

## Current implementation status (2026-11-17)

### Public build
- `v2.0.0-beta.1` is a public GitHub pre-release with an installable debug-signed APK.
- It is not a production-signed release and has not passed physical-device regression testing.
- The beta contains the v2 WebView shell and partial controls, not the complete product roadmap.

### Stability branch: `v2.0.0-stability`
- Replaced the placeholder D-shaped launcher glyph with a custom vector mark based on the approved “The Endless Scroll” direction (phone, swipe trail and hand).
- Reused the new mark in the About page.
- Added an optional local Scroll Pause intervention. When enabled and when the user explicitly enables the Android Accessibility service, it counts only scroll events while Doom Scroll is foregrounded. It does not retrieve window content, screen text, messages, passwords or URLs.
- Scroll Pause defaults off; the default trigger is 12 scroll events within 5 seconds, with a 20-minute cooldown. Both values are configurable.
- Added a recovery path for an Android WebView renderer process termination so a crashed renderer can be recreated instead of taking down the host activity.
- Added unit coverage for Scroll Pause defaults and preference bounds.
- CI and physical-device validation are pending for this branch.

### Partially implemented
- Instagram and Threads remain official website WebViews with separate sessions/navigation histories.
- Feed Firewall is best-effort DOM filtering for visible recommendations, suggested accounts, selected usernames and some navigation entry points. It cannot change server-side ranking and may break when Meta changes its markup.
- Feed Profiles are presets over those same local controls.
- Learning Focus is deterministic visible-text matching, not AI/semantic classification.
- Story muting is session-only.
- Session reminder is a reminder, not a hard stop or usage timer.
- Reduced motion, larger app-shell text and one-handed dock placement are wired to the app shell; high-contrast preference is not yet wired.

### Not implemented / capability-gated
- No DNS/VPN ad-blocking service and no HTTPS interception.
- No native notification listener, notification summaries or quiet-hour notification scheduler.
- No Community Feature Voting backend.
- No smart focus schedule, finite-feed/manual-loading implementation or separate Reels Speed Bump.
- No semantic/AI content classifier.
- No persistent Story mute storage.

### Release gates
- CI must pass on the stability branch.
- Physical-device testing must cover first launch, Instagram and Threads login, cookie/session persistence, switching, Android Back, uploads, camera/microphone permissions, dock gestures, Story muting, Feed Firewall reversibility, Scroll Pause permission and cooldown, and WebView renderer recovery.
- Verify install/upgrade behaviour and accessibility disclosure.
- Do not promote to a stable release until device testing is complete and production signing is configured.
